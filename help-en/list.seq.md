# list.seq

###### generates a sequence of numbers in the form of a list

*available since version:* 0.1

---




[![example](../examples/img/list.seq.jpg)](../examples/pd/list.seq.pd)



## arguments:

* **FROM**
start value<br>
_type:_ float<br>

* **TO**
end value<br>
_type:_ float<br>

* **STEP**
sequence step<br>
_type:_ float<br>





## properties:

* **@closed** (initonly)
Get/set generate closed range sequence<br>
_type:_ flag<br>
_default:_ 0<br>

* **@from** 
Get/set start value<br>
_type:_ float<br>
_default:_ 0<br>

* **@step** 
Get/set sequence step (always positive)<br>
_type:_ float<br>
_default:_ 1<br>

* **@to** 
Get/set end value<br>
_type:_ float<br>
_default:_ 1<br>



## inlets:

* outputs generated list<br>
_type:_ control



## outlets:

* generated sequence<br>
_type:_ control
